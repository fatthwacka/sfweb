How we Skyrocketed a client's followers in just 90 days

See how we crafted a winning strategy for a flagging instagram account.

5 December 2025 • By SlyFox Admin • Case Study

In the world of business social media, a robust follower base is more than just a vanity metric; it's a crucial indicator of brand awareness, audience engagement, and potential reach.

We recently helped a local coffee shop struggling to gain traction on Instagram despite serving exceptional coffee and cultivating a cozy atmosphere.

Why isn't your business profile getting any engagement?

They were averaging only 5-10 new followers per month, and their engagement was minimal. The issue was twofold: picture quality and a lack of human element. All the pictures were taken with a mobile phone and lacked colour and depth, failing to showcase the coffee in an appetising way. Additionally, the shots were entirely product-focused and lacked a human element.

So, we spent just half a day shooting in the coffee shop and captured enough shots to keep their channel thriving for a solid month with fresh content.

The solution: A monthly Pro shoot with automated posting

We implemented a targeted, data-driven strategy that focused on three key areas:

Content Optimization & Diversification: The images lacked personality and identity. We introduced a content calendar that included: Behind-the-Scenes Content: Short videos showcasing the coffee roasting process, barista skills, and daily routines. For example, one video showing a barista crafting latte art garnered significantly more views and shares than static images. User-Generated Content (UGC): We encouraged customers to share their experiences. This boosted engagement and provided authentic social proof. Interactive Content: Polls, quizzes, and Q&A sessions on Instagram Stories to encourage audience participation and gather valuable insights into customer preferences.

Targeted Engagement & Outreach: We identified relevant local Instagram accounts (food bloggers, influencers, community pages) and actively engaged with their content. We also implemented a targeted follow/unfollow strategy, focusing on users who followed similar coffee shops and businesses in the area. This significantly increased their visibility within their target market.

While their photos had good composition and subject interest, they lacked visual clarity and warmth and felt cluttered

What kind of hashtags should I use?

Strategic Hashtag Usage: We researched and implemented a mix of broad, niche, and location-specific hashtags relevant to the coffee industry and the shop's local area. We used tools like Hashtagify to identify trending and related hashtags. For example, instead of only using #coffee, we incorporated #localcoffeeshop, #artisancoffee, #citynamecoffee, and #coffeeshopvibes. The Results: A Blooming Instagram Presence

Within the first 3 months, they experienced a remarkable transformation:

Follower Growth: Their follower count increased by 215%, from 350 to over 1100 followers. Engagement Rate: Their average engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) increased by 180%, indicating a more active and responsive audience. Website Traffic: Website traffic from Instagram increased by 85%, demonstrating the direct impact of their improved social media presence on their business. Customer Feedback: the shop reported a noticeable increase in new customers mentioning they found the shop through Instagram, validating the effectiveness of our strategy.

This case study demonstrates the power of a strategic and data-driven approach to Instagram marketing. By focusing on content optimization, targeted engagement, and strategic hashtag usage, we helped the client achieve significant follower growth and engagement in just 3 months. This success not only boosted their brand awareness but also translated into increased website traffic and, ultimately, more customers walking through their door.
